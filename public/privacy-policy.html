Official Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy for Regional Compass Apps

Last updated: September 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to our suite of regional compass applications, including Hindi Compass (हिंदी कंपास), Marathi Compass (मराठी होकायंत्र), Tamil Compass (தமிழ் திசைகாட்டி), Bengali Compass (বাংলা কম্পাস), Odia Compass (ଓଡ଼ିଆ ଦିଗଦର୍ଶକ), Bhojpuri Compass (भोजपुरी कंपास), and related utility apps (collectively referred to as "the App", "we", "us", or "our").

1. Information We Collect and How It Is Used

We are deeply committed to protecting your privacy. The App is designed as a standalone, offline-first directional and leveling utility. We do NOT collect, transmit, store, sell, or share any personal identifiable information (PII).

A. Device Sensors (Magnetometer, Accelerometer, Gyroscope)

  • Purpose: To calculate real-time 360-degree heading direction, device orientation, pitch, roll, and spirit level status.
  • Data Handling: Sensor data is processed entirely in real-time on your physical device hardware and is discarded instantaneously. It is never logged or transmitted over the network.

B. Location Data (GPS & Network Location)

  • Purpose: When permission is granted, location coordinates (Latitude, Longitude, and Altitude) are used solely on-device to:
    • Calculate True Geographic North offset (Magnetic Declination).
    • Calculate local astronomical Sunrise, Solar Noon, and Sunset times.
    • Display your current elevation (altitude).
  • Data Handling: Location data is processed 100% locally on your device. We do not track your location history, nor do we send your coordinates to any external servers or third-party analytics services.

2. Third-Party Services & Analytics

Our compass applications do not integrate third-party advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, behavioral profilers, or external analytics services that harvest user data.

3. Permissions Required by the App

  • ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION: Used strictly on-device for local sunrise/sunset times and True North computation.
  • VIBRATE: Used to provide haptic feedback when aligning with cardinal directions (can be toggled off in Settings).

4. Children’s Privacy

Our applications do not address anyone under the age of 13, nor do we knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be reflected on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
